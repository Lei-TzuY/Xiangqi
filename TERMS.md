# Terms of Use

Last updated: 2026-09-04

Xiangqi is provided as an experimental Chinese chess game service and open-source software. It is intended for entertainment, education, and software development/testing.

The service may change, reset game state, or be unavailable while under development. It does not currently promise competition-official adjudication for long-check, long-chase, or repetition scenarios. Users should not rely on the prototype as an official tournament arbiter.
